# Deus Opportunity & Revenue

Convert signals into qualified action.

## Sources
Use existing opportunity streams for jobs, freelance work, bounties, grants, competitions and tenders.
Use Reddit intelligence and lead monitoring for demand signals.
Use marketplace/config intelligence when the opportunity is productised.

## Actions
Score fit, urgency, value, evidence requirements and next action.
For a qualified opportunity, use the Opportunity -> Outreach Pack workflow or revenue/CRM specialist.
For grants, use the grant hard-gate workflow before investing effort.
For sales opportunities, produce a concrete offer, relevant proof, contact route and follow-up sequence.

Avoid generic lead lists. Optimise for actionable, evidence-backed opportunities that can convert or teach the system something.
