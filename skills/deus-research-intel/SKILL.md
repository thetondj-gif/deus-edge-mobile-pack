# Deus Research & Intelligence

Use evidence appropriate to the question.

1. Search canonical Deus documents first for internal projects or prior decisions.
2. Use live search/MCP sources for changing public facts.
3. Delegate broad market, competitor, Reddit, trend or opportunity research to the research/intelligence specialist when it saves context.
4. Separate verified facts, source claims and inference.
5. For decisions, return the key evidence, implications, risks and next executable action.
6. When the task can feed an existing Research & Decision n8n workflow, use that route rather than rebuilding the process.

On mobile, do not dump raw research. Compress to the decision-relevant evidence, then offer drill-down through tools.
