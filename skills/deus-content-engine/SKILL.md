# Deus Content Engine

Content should come from real work, tests, configurations, outcomes, products and evidence.

## Modes
- Brand .com: symbolic apparel, Inner Command, philosophy, product and lifestyle content.
- Business / .co.uk: useful commercial insight, proof, lead generation and UK-market content.
- Founder: systems, AI configuration, automation and build lessons without exposing sensitive internals.

## Execution
Use Config Content Hype for config/test-driven content.
Use signal -> YouTube commissioning for video pipeline work.
Use brand/social/writer specialists for channel adaptation.
Use live research when freshness matters.
Reuse one underlying evidence package across article, social, short-form, email and SEO variants.

Do not publish unverifiable performance claims or confidential system details.
